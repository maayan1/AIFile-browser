The PRD still holds up: a clear thesis, recorded author decisions, and phases with testable exit criteria. The three Exercise 1 EARS requirements are now present in EARS form, each with an acceptance test and a carrying FR.

The risk is in how the new pieces interact. FR-6's EARS-03 bullet contradicts FR-17 once Smart Search exists (one high finding). Two medium gaps need an author decision: whether the Preview Panel shows text for files outside Indexed Folders, and what wins when the Relevance Floor can't satisfy both the nonsense-query list and SM-1.

Status after fixes (2026-10-31): all 10 findings are resolved. The high finding and 5 lows were fixed, the author decided the 3 mediums, and 1 low was accepted on purpose. See PRD §12.9.

Exercise 6 checks: EARS requirements present: pass. Every FR testable: pass after the high fix. Readable by a non-engineer: pass. UX pass: done (DESIGN.md, EXPERIENCE.md, 3 mockups); the PRD doesn't link to it yet.

No precedence when the Relevance Floor's two targets conflict

§4.4 FR-17
One fixed floor may not be able to empty every nonsense query and keep SM-1 ≥ 80% at the same time. Decision needed.

§4.2 FR-6, §4.4 FR-17

medium

Does the preview show text for files outside the Indexed Folders?

§3 Supported Document, §4.7 FR-22
The Glossary limits Supported Documents to Indexed Folders, and FR-22 shows text only for Supported Documents, so a DOCX elsewhere gets no text preview. Intent and the link to FR-7's privacy promise are unstated. Decision needed.
Fix: (a) Preview text for any TXT, PDF, DOCX or MD file the user selects, read on demand and never stored, or (b) limit text preview to Indexed Folders and show "Not in an indexed folder" elsewhere.
